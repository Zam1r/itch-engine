# Benchmark results (13 Oct 2026)

All numbers below are copied from `bench/results.json` (generated by `bench/summarize.py` from `bench/results/raw.jsonl`). Nothing is rounded except the speed-up ratios (2 d.p.).

## Environment
- Shared cloud **KVM virtual machine**, 8 vCPU "Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor", 1 thread/core, kernel 6.12.94+. No isolcpus, no turbo/C-state control, other workloads possibly running on the host.
- g++ (Debian 14.2.0-19) 14.2.0, `-O3 -march=native -DNDEBUG -std=c++20`, no LTO.
- Single thread pinned with `taskset -c 2`. TSC measured at 2.4000 ticks/ns.

## Input
- NASDAQ TotalView-ITCH 5.0 public sample `01302019.NASDAQ_ITCH50.gz` (30 Jan 2019) from emi.nasdaq.com. **Slice**: the first 400 MiB of the 4.76 GB gzip → 1,035,054,410 bytes, **33,063,085 messages**, timestamps 03:03:59 → 09:46:03 ET (pre-market plus the first ~16 minutes of regular trading). Not a full day.
- 8,714 distinct stock locates; **8,694 symbols had at least one order**. 1,566,548 live orders at end of slice.
- Type counts: A 13,788,235 · D 12,688,783 · U 2,490,561 · X 1,353,658 · I 1,051,262 · F 858,388 · E 486,471 · L 193,725 · P 105,963 · C 11,155 · Y 8,734 · H 8,718 · Q 8,713 · R 8,714 · S 3 · J 1 · V 1.

## Method
- `throughput`: decode + book update for the whole slice from memory, wall clock (steady_clock), no per-message timing.
- `latency`: each message timed individually around `dispatch()` (decode + book update) with `lfence; rdtsc` … `rdtscp; lfence`. The median empty-region cost (46 cycles) is subtracted. The first 100,000 messages are processed but excluded as warm-up. 1 ns histogram bins.
- 5 runs per book, alternating fast/map. Reported: median of runs [min–max].
- Correctness: `itch_verify` replays both books together and compares every symbol's full depth (price, qty, order count per level) every 5M messages and at the end. Result: **113,114 side comparisons, 0 mismatches**.

Notes:
- Max values are not meaningful latency figures on a shared VM; the fast book's max is most likely a host/OS interruption. The ~0.2 s std::map max is most likely an `unordered_map` rehash.
- The fast book's 84 hot-path allocations come from a few symbols whose price-level vector outgrew its 512-level reservation (17,426 other allocations are the reservations made on Stock Directory messages). So it is "near-zero", not strictly zero.
- p50/p99 here are **in-process decode + book update per message**. They are not wire-to-wire or tick-to-trade numbers.
- Not done: `perf stat`/flame graphs (perf isn't available on this VM).
